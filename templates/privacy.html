Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

Purpose and operator

Debate Documents is operated by the owner of the connected debate Gmail inbox for members of their private Discord server. It copies incoming round emails and speech documents into that server. Contact the server administrator for questions about this policy.

Google data accessed

With the inbox owner's authorization, the service uses read-only Gmail access to read message and conversation identifiers, labels, sender details, subject lines, timestamps, message text, and attachments. It uses Gmail mailbox history and notifications to find newly received email.

How data is used and shared

Sender details, subjects, timestamps, message text, and attachments are sent to the configured Discord forum, where members with access may read, download, or copy them. Google Cloud processes the data to run the integration. Discord processes and stores the posted messages and files under its own terms and privacy policy. No email is forwarded to another Discord destination unless the operator changes the configuration.

The service does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Email text and attachment bytes are processed temporarily in server memory. The application stores delivery records, message and conversation identifiers, Discord message identifiers, timestamps, and operational status in Google Cloud Firestore. These records are retained until the operator deletes them. Operational logs may contain request metadata; the application does not intentionally log email bodies or attachment contents.

Google authorization credentials and the Discord webhook are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager. Discord posts and files remain until deleted there. Original emails remain in Gmail under the inbox owner's control. Pausing forwarding or revoking authorization does not automatically delete existing Discord posts or delivery records.

Your controls

The inbox owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. The operator can pause forwarding, delete stored credentials and delivery records, and delete posts from Discord. Contact the private Discord server administrator to request access, correction, or deletion. Copies independently downloaded by other members may need to be deleted by those members.

Security and changes

The service uses HTTPS, cloud access controls, protected credentials, and authenticated notification handlers. Discord server permissions determine who can see forwarded documents. The operator should review this policy whenever the integration's behavior or audience changes.
