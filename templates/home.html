Speech documents, organized by round.

Debate Documents connects one dedicated Gmail inbox to a private Discord forum for a small group of college debaters.

When the inbox receives an email, the service copies its sender information, message text, and attachments to the Discord conversation for that email chain. Friends with access to the forum can follow the round and download the original evidence documents.

How access works

The inbox owner explicitly authorizes read-only Gmail access. This permission is used to detect incoming messages and retrieve their contents and attachments. The service does not send email or delete messages.

This is a privately operated integration, not a public signup service. Access to forwarded documents is controlled by the Discord server's permissions. Emails and documents are never displayed on this public website.

For support or a deletion request, contact the administrator of the private Discord server that uses this integration.
